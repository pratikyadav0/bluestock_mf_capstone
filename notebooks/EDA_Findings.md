# EDA Findings Summary — Bluestock Fintech

## 10 Key Findings from Exploratory Data Analysis

### 1. NAV Growth Trends
Small Cap funds (SBI Small Cap, Nippon Small Cap) showed the highest NAV appreciation
from 2022-2026, with 3-year CAGR exceeding 22%, significantly outperforming Large Cap
funds which averaged ~15% CAGR.

### 2. AUM Concentration
SBI Mutual Fund dominates the industry with Rs. 12.50 Lakh Crore AUM (Dec 2025),
followed by ICICI Prudential (Rs. 10.74L Cr) and HDFC (Rs. 9.30L Cr). The top 3 AMCs
control over 39% of industry AUM.

### 3. SIP Inflow Milestone
Monthly SIP inflows hit an all-time high of Rs. 31,002 Crore in December 2025, reflecting
a 34% YoY growth. The consistent upward trend from Rs. 13,000 Cr (2022) demonstrates
India's deepening equity culture.

### 4. Category Preferences
Large Cap and Flexi Cap categories attracted the highest net inflows. Small Cap funds
saw volatile inflows with periods of negative net flows during market corrections,
while Liquid funds showed consistent but modest inflows.

### 5. Investor Demographics
The 26-35 age group constitutes the largest investor segment, contributing ~35% of total
transaction volume. Average SIP amounts increase with age, with the 46-55 group showing
the highest average at approximately Rs. 12,000-15,000 per month.

### 6. Geographic Concentration
Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi NCR account for over 50% of total transaction volume.
T30 (Top 30) cities contribute approximately 65% of all investments, with B30 cities
showing faster growth rates but smaller absolute volumes.

### 7. Folio Growth
Total MF folios grew from ~13.26 Crore (2022) to 26.12 Crore (Dec 2025), representing
a near-doubling in 4 years. Equity folios grew fastest, driven by SIP adoption.

### 8. NAV Correlation
Large Cap funds show high correlation (0.85-0.95) with each other and with Nifty indices.
Small Cap and Mid Cap funds are less correlated with Large Caps (0.65-0.80), offering
diversification benefits in a multi-fund portfolio.

### 9. Sector Concentration
BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, Insurance) is the most heavily weighted sector across
equity fund portfolios, followed by IT and Automobiles. Some sectoral funds show high
concentration risk (HHI > 2500).

### 10. Transaction Patterns
SIP transactions account for approximately 60% of all transactions by count but only
~25% by value (due to smaller individual amounts). Lumpsum investments dominate by value.
UPI has become the most popular payment mode (40%+), overtaking Net Banking.
